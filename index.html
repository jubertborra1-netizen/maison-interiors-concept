One wall, six finishes

Scroll to lay each finish over the last. Every one is mixed and applied by hand, on site.

  • Lime plaster

    The base coat: a breathable lime render, trowelled by hand so the surface keeps a soft movement.

  • Venetian stucco

    Thin layers of tinted stucco, burnished until the wall takes on the depth and sheen of polished stone.

  • Marble effect

    Veining painted in glazes over a pale ground, for columns, panels and feature walls where real stone is too heavy or too costly.

  • Gold leaf and metallics

    Leaf laid square by square over a red bole, then softly aged so the ground shows at the edges.

  • Fabric-matched finish

    Colour and texture matched to the room's curtains and upholstery, with an optional stencilled pattern in the same tones.

  • Murals and trompe l'oeil

    Painted scenes and architectural illusions, from a single arch on a feature wall to a full ceiling.
